# Code of conduct

> [English](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md) · [中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

Plurora is an open platform. People who ask questions, report defects, submit code, or write documentation should be able to work here without harassment.

## Our standards

Encouraged:

- Be direct, specific, and checkable toward both people and code;
- Assume good faith, but argue from facts rather than identity;
- Welcome different experience levels, languages, and product directions;
- Aim criticism at behavior and design, not at people.

Unacceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks;
- Publishing someone else's private information;
- Sustained disruption, or turning security-vulnerability details into a public performance;
- Dismissing the above as “just a joke.”

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review in this repository, and to related spaces where someone is acting as a Plurora maintainer.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the channel in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.en.md), or contact the maintainers directly when the matter is not a security vulnerability. Maintainers may warn, hide or remove content, and restrict participation when needed.

Maintainers are held to the same standard.
