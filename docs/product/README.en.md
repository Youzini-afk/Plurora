# Product

> [English](./README.en.md) · [中文](./README.md)

These documents separate Plurora's overall product responsibility from optional product profiles. The official distribution should be complete, usable, and aggressively polished, but its organization must not become mandatory ontology for the whole platform.

- [`PLATFORM_PRODUCT_MODEL.md`](PLATFORM_PRODUCT_MODEL.en.md) — users, lifecycles, usability principles, and the product boundary of Project and the official shell
- [`PLAY_CREATION_MODEL.md`](PLAY_CREATION_MODEL.en.md) — the optional play-creation product profile and the current Home / Play / Forge / Assist language
